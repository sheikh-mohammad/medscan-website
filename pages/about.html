About MED SCAN

A reading aid that turns a doctor’s handwriting into a clear, safe medicine schedule — in English, Roman Urdu or Urdu.

Read the scribble

Snap a photo of any prescription. MED SCAN cleans up the image and reads the medicine names and dosing codes.

Decode the dose

1+0+1, BD, TDS, HS, SOS are explained in plain language — zero guessing, zero wrong assumptions.

Your schedule

Reminders match your prescription exactly, for exactly as many days as the doctor wrote.

Your language

Switch between English, Roman Urdu and Urdu with one tap. The schedule text stays the same in every language.

Important notice

MED SCAN is a reading aid, not a medical diagnosis. Always confirm medicines and doses with your pharmacist or doctor before taking anything. We never share your log — it stays on your phone.
