Why MED SCAN

Handwritten prescriptions are hard to read. One misread dose can be dangerous. MED SCAN turns the scribble into a clear schedule you can trust.

Reads handwriting

AI scans the prescription and extracts medicine names and dosing codes.

Verifies medicines

Every name is checked against a verified database before reminders are set.

Never misses a dose

Alarms for every dose, for exactly as many days as prescribed.
